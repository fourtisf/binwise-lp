## Assumptions (read before trusting the numbers)

- **Fees:** only the pool's base fee is counted (DLMM's extra volatility fee is ignored, so fees err low). Our share of each trade = our liquidity in the active bin ÷ (pool active-bin liquidity + ours), with the pool's active bin assumed to hold 5% of TVL. Protocol fee share taken from each pool's config.
- **TVL:** today's TVL is used for every hour (historical TVL isn't available from the API).
- **Costs:** 105,000 lamports per transaction + 10,000 lamport tip, SOL at $152; swaps pay 10 bps plus size impact. New bin-array rent (0.0714 SOL) is not charged.
- **Volatility** uses hourly candles here; the live agent uses 5-minute candles.

No strategy is guaranteed to profit. Past results don't guarantee future returns.
